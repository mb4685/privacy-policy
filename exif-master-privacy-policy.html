ExifMaster Pro - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

ExifMaster Pro ("the app") reads and edits metadata of photos and videos on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: your files stay on your device, the app has no accounts and no analytics, and the free version shows one banner ad.

1. Your photos and metadata

Files you open are processed only on your device. The app does not upload photos, videos or their metadata to any server. History (recent files, batch edit log) and settings are stored locally and can be deleted in Settings, option "Clear history".

2. Location

The app asks for your device location only when you tap a button that needs it ("Use device location", "Center on me"). The position is used on the device to fill GPS fields or center the map and is not stored or sent anywhere. The "media location" permission only lets the app read the GPS data that is already inside photos you selected.

3. Maps and address search

Maps are OpenStreetMap tiles. When you view or download map tiles, the tile server receives your IP address and the requested map area. Address search sends the text you type to Nominatim (OpenStreetMap). These services are governed by the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy. Downloaded tiles are cached on the device and can be removed in Settings.

4. Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows a small banner ad provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the advertising ID, IP address and device information to serve and measure ads, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app shows banner ads only: no pop-up, interstitial or full-screen ads. The premium version has no ads and does not contain the advertising SDK.

5. Diagnostic data (optional)

Collecting diagnostic data is off by default. If you turn it on, crash reports are saved on your device. They are sent only if you choose "Send diagnostic data": the app then prepares a ZIP file (app version, device model, Android version, language, batch job log, crash reports) and opens your e-mail app so you can review and send it to the authors. Nothing is sent automatically. Turning the option off deletes stored crash reports.

6. Notifications

The app shows a notification with the progress of a long batch edit that you started. Notifications contain no personal data.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
