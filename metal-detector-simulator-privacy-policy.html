Privacy Policy

Metal Detector Simulator (pl.gmapk.metaldetector.app) · Last updated: 25 October 2026

Metal Detector Simulator is an entertainment app that turns the magnetometer built into your phone into a playful metal detector. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information the app does not collect

The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. It does not ask for your name, e-mail address, location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone, and it does not send any of these anywhere.

Sensor data

Readings from the magnetometer are processed only on your device, only while the detector screen is open, and are never stored or transmitted. Session totals (scanning time, number of targets, strongest field) live in memory and are discarded when the app closes.

Settings

Your choices — sensitivity, sound, vibration, theme, language and similar options — are saved locally on the device. If Android backup is enabled on your phone, the operating system may include these settings in your device backup with Google. The app itself never reads them back from anywhere else.

Advertising

The app shows a single banner at the bottom of the screen, served by Google AdMob. There are no pop-up, interstitial or full-screen ads. To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as the device advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, app interactions with the ad and approximate location derived from the IP address. This processing is done by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and personal data

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on the device).

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load the banner ad.
  • Vibrate — for haptic feedback while detecting.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
