PassForge – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

PassForge ("the app") is a password-recovery tool for encrypted archive files that you own or are authorised to test. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we do not collect

PassForge performs all password recovery locally on your device. The archive file you select, any wordlist you provide, the passwords tried, and any password found are processed only in device memory and are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server. The developer has no account system and no analytics.

Files you select

The app reads a file only after you explicitly pick it through the system file picker. It accesses that file solely to attempt recovery and does not scan or index any other files on your device.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version displays a single banner advertisement supplied by Google AdMob. To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as a device advertising identifier, coarse information about your device, and ad interaction data, in accordance with Google's policies. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The premium version contains no ads and does not include the ads SDK, and requires no internet access.

Permissions

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE – keeps a long-running, user-initiated recovery alive while you switch apps or turn off the screen.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS – shows recovery progress and the result. Requested only when you start a recovery.
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (free version only) – required by the AdMob SDK to load the banner ad.

Children

PassForge is a utility tool and is not directed at children.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer at the email address listed on the app's Google Play store page.
