Privacy Policy

Secure File Eraser (Free and Premium) · Last updated: 9 November 2026

Secure File Eraser ("the app") is developed by GMAPK44. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. In short: the app works entirely on your device, and the only third-party data processing happens through the advertising SDK in the free version.

Files you choose to erase

The app reads and overwrites only the files and folders you select through the Android system file picker. Their contents never leave your device and are never sent to the developer or anyone else. The app has no access to other files.

Data stored on your device

  • Settings such as language, theme and behaviour switches.
  • Operation history (date, number of files, size, method, result and, if you allow it, file paths). You can switch history off and delete it at any time from the app.
  • Schedules you create, including the folders they target.

This data stays in the app's private storage, is not backed up to the cloud by the app, and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Diagnostic data

The app never sends diagnostic data automatically. If you tap "Send diagnostic data" in Settings, the app prepares a report and opens your own mail app with the report attached and addressed to the developer. The report contains the app version, Android version, device model, language and theme, and, only if you enabled "Collect diagnostic data", recent log lines of the app itself. It does not contain your files or file names. Nothing is sent unless you send the e-mail yourself.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address and basic device information, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. The Premium version contains no ads and no advertising SDK.

Permissions

  • Notifications – to show progress and a Cancel button while erasing.
  • Foreground service (data sync) – so a long erase keeps running when you leave the app.
  • Run at startup – to re-arm your schedules after a restart.
  • Internet – used only by the advertising SDK in the free version.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Data sharing and sale

The developer does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Apart from the advertising described above, no data is transmitted from the app.
